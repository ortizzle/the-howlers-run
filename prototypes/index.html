Merit: the new point system

I · Show UpActive Days, the Weekly Standard, and the chain multiplier
≈ 45% of a typical week
II · EffortMinutes × intensity. Tread, bike, yoga and hikes all count the same.
≈ 20%
III · ProgressWeight trend (opt-in, private) or personal records
≈ 25%
IV · Habits3 daily habits you pick (steps, water, sleep…)
≈ 10%

The rules

Active Day: 20+ min of any activity+10
Weekly Standard: 4 Active Days, Mon–Sun+40
Chain: each straight Standard week adds +10% to that week's Show Up + Effort. Missing a week drops one link, not all of them.up to ×1.5
Effort: per 10 min, Easy / Steady / Hard (daily cap 20)1 / 2 / 3
Weekly weigh-in+5
New low: each 0.5% of starting weight, measured on a smoothed trend line instead of single weigh-ins+25
Pace cap: no more than 1% credited per week, so a crash diet earns nothing extra—
Milestones: 5% · 10% · 15%+100 · 150 · 200
Holiday Hold: trend on Jan 4 no more than 0.5% above Dec 1+100
Fitness track (instead of the scale): personal record, max 2 per week+25
Habits: up to 3 per day+2 each

Why it's built this way

  • Consistency beats intensity. Four 20-minute days earn more than one heroic 2-hour session. The daily effort cap and the Standard make sure of that.
  • Percent, not pounds. Progress is measured as a % of your own starting weight, so it's fair across very different bodies. Pounds are visible only to you, and sharing your % with the pack is optional.
  • Trend, not the daily scale. Water weight can swing the scale 2–3 lbs in a day. Points come from new lows on the smoothed trend, so one bad weigh-in can't cost you anything.
  • Healthy pace only. Credit is capped at 1% per week. Losing faster than that earns nothing extra, and the remaining credit unlocks over the following weeks.
  • Kids stay off the scale. Sedona and River default to the Fitness track (PRs, minutes, habits). Weight tracking is opt-in for adults.
  • December is the hardest month, so the first challenge is to hold your weight through the holidays. Maintaining counts as a win.
  • Forgiving chain. A missed week drops your chain by one link, not to zero, so one sick week doesn't wipe out a month of streak.
